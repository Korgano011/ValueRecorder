Value Recorder Support

Screen recordings with tiny files, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Need help or found a problem? Email operafan01@gmail.com. Please include your device (for example iPhone or Mac) and what you were doing when it happened.

Recording on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Value Recorder and choose a size. 120p makes the smallest files.
  2. Tap the record button, choose Value Recorder, then tap Start Broadcast.
  3. To stop, tap the red indicator at the top of the screen, or use Control Center.
  4. Open Value Recorder again. The new recording appears in the list.

To include your voice, turn on the microphone in the broadcast window before you tap Start Broadcast.

Recording on Mac

  1. Choose a size and what to record (the whole screen or one window), then click Record.
  2. The first time, macOS asks for permission. Turn on Value Recorder in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, then reopen the app.
  3. Stop or pause from the record button in the menu bar.

Making files smaller

  • Pick a smaller size: 120p or 240p.
  • Lower the frames per second in Settings. 10–15 is plenty for slides, documents and apps.
  • Choose HEVC, which makes files about 30% smaller than H.264.
  • Use the MP3 & MP4 Converter to shrink videos you already have, or keep just the sound as an MP3.

Audio to Text

Audio to Text turns what was said in a recording into notes. It runs on your device, so it works without an internet connection, but the first use may need your device’s speech language to be installed. If it doesn’t start, allow Speech Recognition for Value Recorder in Settings › Privacy & Security.

Where are my recordings?

  • iPhone and iPad: Files app › On My iPhone (or iPad) › Value Recorder.
  • Mac: Movies › Value Recorder, unless you chose another folder in Settings.
  • Recordings you save to the Repository are kept in its own folder, which you can also change in Settings.
